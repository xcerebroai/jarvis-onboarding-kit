# Hot-Lead Escalation

Notify the owner IMMEDIATELY when any of these appear:
- Seller wants to sell NOW or sign today
- Vacant property
- Behind on payments / foreclosure notice received
- Non-paying tenant
- Inherited property / probate situation
- Flexible on price ("just make me an offer")
- Asks for an offer today or a callback
- Offers property access
- Mentions a competing offer

## On any trigger, in this order:
1. Summarize the lead (03_lead-intake.md format)
2. Mark it HOT
3. Apply the hot tag in Jarvis CRM (per 10_jarvis-crm-rules.md — additive endpoint only)
4. Move to the correct pipeline stage
5. Notify the owner on [Telegram/channel] with the summary + recommended action
6. Do NOT make any commitment to the seller while waiting
