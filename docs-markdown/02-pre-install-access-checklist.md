# JARVIS Agent — Pre-Install Access Checklist

**Complete this BEFORE your install call.** Every unchecked box costs us 10–15 minutes of your call time on password resets instead of building your agent.

---

## Devices & Apps

- [ ] I have a computer I can install software on (Windows or Mac), and I'll be at it during the call
- [ ] Telegram is installed on my **phone** and I'm logged in
- [ ] Telegram is installed on my **computer** and I'm logged in with the same account
- [ ] I can join a Zoom call with screen share from that computer

## Accounts & Logins (test each login TODAY, not on call day)

- [ ] I can log into my **JARVIS account** (we sent your credentials in your welcome email)
- [ ] I can log into my **Jarvis CRM** account
- [ ] I can log into my AI account:
  - ChatGPT users: [ ] I can log into chat.openai.com
  - Claude users: [ ] I have my payment method ready — we'll set up a usage-based account together on the call (takes 5 minutes, you control the spend limit)
- [ ] I know my email password (some steps send verification codes)

## Business Materials (have these ready to copy/paste or upload)

- [ ] My buy box in writing (cities, zips, property types, minimum spread)
- [ ] My seller scripts (cold call, inbound, follow-up)
- [ ] My follow-up text messages
- [ ] My offer formula
- [ ] A list of automations currently running in my CRM (or just tell us on the call)
- [ ] My do-not-say list (anything the agent should never promise or claim)

## Team

- [ ] If a VA or team member will use the agent, they're invited to the call (optional but recommended)

## What NOT to do before the call

- Don't create any bots, keys, or tokens ahead of time — we do that together so nothing gets lost
- Don't share passwords with us by text or email — you'll type your own logins during the call

---

**Call length:** 60–90 minutes. By the end, your JARVIS Agent will be live in your Telegram, trained on your business, connected to your CRM, and tested with you driving.
