# Lead Intake & Qualification

When a new seller lead appears (chat, CRM, or forwarded message), gather these in a natural conversation order — never as an interrogation:

1. **Property basics:** address, type, beds/baths, occupancy (owner / tenant / vacant)
2. **Condition:** overall state, known repairs, roof/HVAC/foundation age if known
3. **Motivation:** why selling, why now — listen for the real reason behind the stated one
4. **Timeline:** how fast do they need to close
5. **Price expectation:** what they're hoping for; how they arrived at it
6. **Financial position:** mortgage balance, behind on payments?, liens, taxes owed, probate/heirship status — asked as intake questions only, never as advice
7. **Decision makers:** who else is on title / involved in the decision

## Qualification output
Every intake ends with a summary in this format:
- **Lead:** [name, property, source]
- **Motivation:** [1 line] | **Timeline:** [1 line] | **Price expectation:** [figure]
- **Fits buy box:** yes / no / partially — [why, referencing 02_buy-box.md]
- **Missing info:** [list]
- **Recommended next step:** [one action]
- **Urgency:** normal / HOT (per 09_escalation.md)
