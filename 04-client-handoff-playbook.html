Owner's Playbook

How to run your JARVIS Agent every day

Your JARVIS Agent is live. It knows your business, your buy box, your scripts, and your CRM. This playbook shows you how to get money out of it every single day.

One rule: use JARVIS like an operator, not a chatbot. Specific prompts get specific results.

Your Daily Routine (10 minutes, every morning)

Send these to JARVIS in Telegram, in order:

  1. What should I focus on today in my wholesaling business?
  2. Review my leads and tell me who needs follow-up first.
  3. Write my follow-up messages for the top leads.
  4. Summarize any hot leads and tell me what action to take next.

End of day:

  1. Update my CRM notes from today's calls.
    (paste your call notes)
  2. What's still open that I should handle tomorrow?

Your Weekly Routine (Friday or Sunday, 20 minutes)

  • Review my pipeline and tell me where money is stuck.
  • Which leads should I revive this week?
  • Which sellers need a stronger follow-up?
  • What should my acquisitions focus be this week?
  • What should my dispo focus be this week?
  • What SOPs are missing from my business?

What JARVIS Does Best

  • Seller lead intake — paste any lead, get a summary, motivation read, missing questions, and next steps
  • Follow-up writing — texts and emails in your voice, matched to where the lead is
  • Appointment prep — walk in knowing what to ask and what the seller cares about
  • Offer prep — runs your formula, flags what's missing before you make a number
  • CRM updates — creates and updates contacts, notes, tags, and pipeline stages in your Jarvis CRM
  • Buyer outreach — dispo blasts and buyer follow-up drafts
  • Team training — your VAs can ask it "how do we handle X here?" and get YOUR process

Prompt Quality = Result Quality

Bad: "Help me with leads."

Better: "Review this seller lead, tell me if they fit my buy box, and write the next follow-up text."

Best: "Based on my buy box and SOPs, review this seller lead. Tell me if it's worth pursuing, what questions I still need to ask, what pipeline stage it belongs in, what tag it should have, and exactly what I should do next."

Always paste the actual lead details. JARVIS can't read your mind — but it has read your SOPs.

Copy-Paste Prompt Bank

Lead review: "Here's a new seller lead: [paste details]. Does it fit my buy box? What's the motivation? What am I missing? What's the next step?"

Ghosted seller: "Write a follow-up text for a seller who ghosted me after our first call. Property: [address]. Last contact: [when]. Keep it in my voice."

Appointment prep: "I have a seller appointment at [address] tomorrow. Motivation: [x]. Prep me: what to ask, what to listen for, and how to frame my offer."

Offer breakdown: "Help me explain my cash offer to this seller in plain language: ARV [x], repairs [x], my offer [x]."

Pipeline cleanup: "Help me clean up my pipeline — identify dead leads, follow-up leads, and hot leads, and tell me what tags and stages to fix."

Dead lead revival: "Write a revival message for leads that went cold 60+ days ago. Angle: checking in, no pressure, new offer possible."

Buyer blast: "Draft a buyer blast for this deal: [address, ARV, repairs, price, access details]."

VA delegation: "Create a task list for my VA for today based on my open leads."

What JARVIS Will Always Do

  • Escalate hot leads to you immediately (seller ready to sign, foreclosure, vacant, inherited, flexible on price)
  • Ask before anything gets sent to a real seller or buyer
  • Stay inside your buy box and your do-not-say list
  • Keep notes and summaries clean in your CRM

What JARVIS Will Never Do

  • Promise a seller an offer or closing date
  • Quote a definitive property value
  • Give legal or tax advice
  • Send offers or contracts without your approval

That's by design. You're the closer. JARVIS is your operations team.
